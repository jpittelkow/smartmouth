## Session: build step 2, Trivia v1
**Done**
- **Trivia rules** (`packages/games/trivia/src/server.ts`, pure): 8 rounds. Each band answers its own question (the TV shows the
  oldest band's question, and phones with a different question show it); the server refuses late answers; answering
  reveals early once everyone is in. Little turns every other round (no timer; the TV remote via `answerFor`, or a kid-mode phone;
  helpers get +25; a wrong pick still gets +25). Handicapped points (×3 down to ×1, with a speed bonus for Tweens and up).
  Spice turned down mid-game skips spicier questions. The final always makes Littles superstars.
- **Platform:** `admin:start/next/end` and `game:intent`. `gameRuntime.ts` picks questions per band from the library
  (live, in season, spice, not asked tonight, least recently asked) and records every play in the stats. Timers live in ws.ts.
  Players who join or leave, and spice changes, keep the game in step.
- **Screens:** TV (intro, question with a countdown ring, reveal, the Little turn with D-pad focus, final with superstars,
  and a Back menu) and phone (answers, locked in, result, kid mode, helper guesses, final, Next/End for the admin).
  Clocks sync to the server's time.
- **Tests:** 37 server tests (9 for the rules, plus a full-game socket test). A browser run played all 8 rounds with a TV, a
  grown-up, a kid and a Little, including turns by remote and by phone, then back to the lobby, with no page errors.
