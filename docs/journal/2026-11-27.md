## Snake Manor: generated effects, and bots that argue

**Done**
- Effects generated locally with Stable Audio Open 1.0 on the RTX 3090: `tools/sounds/sfx_local.py` reads
  `tools/sounds/sfx-cues.json` (16 cues, each with what it's for and a sound-only prompt). Each take takes about
  12 s. Audition page with 3 takes per cue: https://play.303.social/media/sounds/generated/index.html (owner's
  picks pending).
- Table talk is a public rules intent (`talk`, only for the living, during debate/vote/voteReveal/endVote). The TV
  shows an "At the table" feed and phones get a say box.
- Thinking bots: `botBrain.ts` builds prompts from the bot's own player view only (a test proves a Guest's prompt
  doesn't change when the Snakes are swapped). Each bot gets a temper so they disagree. Every line is checked (spice,
  a Snake never outs a partner, cut at a sentence or clause). `botMinds.ts` decides when to ask the model, and ws.ts
  times the talk: two lines per bot per debate at tables of up to six bots.
- Votes and Snake night picks go to the model. Simple rules fill any gap or failure.
- Model `BOT_MODEL` (default `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5`); `BOT_AI=off` switches it off. Budget is 200 calls per room.
- Live run on play.303.social, 1 human seat + 5 bots: they answered each other by name, and the votes went through.

**Learned**
- Haiku ignores length limits about half the time. A cut with "…" makes the other bots remark on it ("you cut off
  mid-thought"), so cut at a sentence or clause instead.
- Without tempers, every bot piles onto the same point.

**Next**
