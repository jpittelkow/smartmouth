On the phones

Drawn at 390 × 844. Buttons are 64 px or taller. The player's tincture tints the top of their screen, except at night.

The identical night

The rule the game rests on: from across the room, these two phones must look the same.

  • Same header, same dim palette, same text box height, same two-column name grid, same Lock button in the same place.
  • No tincture tint at night: a green Snake tint or a gold Guest tint would give the game away at a glance.
  • Locking does not end the screen. Both stay up until the server's deadline, then dim together.
  • No sound or vibration on any phone at night. The TV makes the noise.

Open choices in these mockups

Decisions these drafts made that the design doc didn't. Each one is yours to change.

  • A serif book face instead of Atkinson Hyperlegible. It carries the period feel. Since this game is Tweens and up, legibility for new readers matters less, but it should be tested at 3 m on the office TV.
  • Coats of arms replace avatar blobs for this game only. The seat's colour becomes a heraldic tincture with a fixed charge.
  • Answer marks become crown, key, rose and sword, each with a Roman numeral, so the cartoon star, heart, moon and bolt stay with trivia.
  • The pot is counted in crowns.
  • Salty's grin is replaced by a wax seal with his initial while he plays the Lord of the Manor. The seal could pulse with his voice the way the grin does.
  • Player names in these drafts are made up (Agnes, Bertram, Cecily and the rest), per the repo's privacy rule.
